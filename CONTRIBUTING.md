# Contributing

## What is most wanted

**Field reports.** A job that reported success while producing nothing, in a shape the catalog does not
already cover. Include:

- what the job was supposed to produce,
- what the status said,
- how long it went unnoticed,
- what finally revealed it.

The mechanism matters more than the stack. `honest-automation/references/failure-catalog.md` is the
target file.

## Rules for anything factual

1. **Cite the primary source.** Vendor docs, man pages, the paper itself — not a blog summarizing them.
2. **State the limit of the guarantee.** Every source note ends with what the source does *not* promise.
   A citation without that boundary is half a citation.
3. **No orphan numbers.** A figure with no traceable path to its origin does not go in, however often
   it is repeated elsewhere.
4. **Verify links before submitting.** Dead or invented URLs are the fastest way to make a reference
   file worthless.

## Style

Skill bodies are dense on purpose — they are read by a model under a token budget. Prefer a table over
a paragraph, a mechanism over an adjective, and a real incident over a hypothetical one.

## Scope

These skills are about *unattended* work. Anything that assumes a human is watching the terminal
belongs somewhere else.
